# Unslop

Use for prose, docs, comments, commit messages, and responses.

1. Cut filler, puffery, vague attribution, hedging, and chatbot phrases.
2. Prefer facts, active voice, short sentences, and plain words.
3. Avoid synonym cycling, forced lists, decorative headings, emojis, and title case.
4. Avoid em dashes, curly quotes, and needless colons or bold text. Use ASCII punctuation.
5. Preserve intent and tone. Do not add unsupported claims.
6. Cut anything generic or obviously machine-written.

Do not alter source syntax or required literals. Keep comments only for non-obvious constraints.
